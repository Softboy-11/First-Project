About us

Yomade is a professional who takes her work seriously — trustworthy, honest, and genuinely passionate about her business. Her experience speaks for itself.

She has been in business since she was a teenager, learning from her own entrepreneurial mother. After completing her undergraduate studies and earning a B.Sc, she became a certified estate manager. Today she runs two ventures: Yomade Stores and Yomade Properties.

Our mission is to keep satisfying clients with quality products and reliable management services. Our vision is to reach more people across Ibadan and beyond.

We are open to everyone, with responsive customer care always ready to assist. We have also worked with:

  • Adron Homes
  • Sweet Estate
  • Sunnytex Properties
Read our full story

Yomade Enterprises was founded in 2005 by Yomade, driven by a passion for providing quality products and services to her community. Over the years the company has grown steadily, becoming a trusted name in Ibadan with a consistent focus on customer satisfaction, excellence and integrity.
